The best way to understand how to pair your watch and configure haptic alerts is through our official YouTube channel:

## 🐛 Bug Reports
If you found a real error in this Wear OS app, please open a **GitHub Issue** in this repository. 
*Please search existing issues before opening a new one.*

## ❌ What is NOT support?
- We do not provide support for non-Android/iOS based smartwatches.
- Please do not use Issues for "How to" questions.
